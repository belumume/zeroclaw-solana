# GOAL — ZeroClaw Solana bounty (4k payload for /goal; master is .claude/GOAL.md)

Master: `.claude/GOAL.md`; the full-amplitude ambition is
`~/DEV/standing-excellence-bar.md`, already `@`-imported into every session. Read those, not a
paraphrase of them.

## Win condition
Win the Superteam / ZeroClaw Solana bounty with a submission that has no second in sight across
every scored axis and every tier. Not "places well" — defines the category, such that no entrant
focusing on a single track comes near it. It spans what a single-track competitor cannot: custody
design, machine-commerce, DePIN, reproducibility, craft, each at the ceiling, in one coherent body
of work.

## Mandate
Ambition is sized to what is POSSIBLE, never to the ask, the clock, the session window, or the
compute budget. Constraint shapes scheduling, never ambition, novelty, or grit.

Full agency is delegated. The user is a physical hand for reach the agent genuinely lacks — a
login, a purchase, a phone camera, a keypress — not a warden to report to or hand settleable
decisions back to. Decide on objectively-best plus non-degrading, and execute.

Own every end of every stack at once: correctness AND security AND craft AND the demo AND the
docs, to the ceiling.

## What the brief requires (the letter governs)
A showcase post in #solana-bounty carrying: a working use case (real agent, real channel, real
Solana job, YOURS RUNNING, GitHub-linked); a video at or under 3 minutes, no slides; a reproducible
write-up (what, who, features, custody plus threat model, reproduction links, secrets redacted);
and a prompt-injection transcript for anything touching funds. No registry PRs during the bounty.

Scored: use-case 30, safety and custody 25, craft 20 (correct layering counts — a Tier-1 solution
to a Tier-1 problem beats unnecessary WASM), reproducibility 15, showcase 10. X build-in-public
logs break ties. Depth beats breadth. Originality counts double. Brazil-first flows especially
welcome.

Re-clear `docs/COMPLIANCE-AUDIT.md` against the letter, never against memory, and re-run
`.tools/fetch-listing.py` at session start — the sponsor can rewrite the brief again.

## The through-line
Deny-by-default custody where the on-chain program, not the plugin and not the LLM, bounds the
agent. A DePIN node that signs its own readings on-chain AND sells them per request, so the machine
earns its own gas. The correct-layering discipline applied to our own work, including demoting a
plugin to a skill when the tier test says so, and showing the reasoning. Reproducibility complete
enough that a stranger runs it in an evening. Every claim empirical, root-caused, byte-validated,
no overclaim.

## Standing loops
Research-grounded and empirical always. Root-cause, root-fix, root-test. Convergence is a clean
independent pass, not a feeling. Confidence in capability, never in unverified assertions.

Every inflection goes to `docs/BUILD-JOURNAL.md` — it feeds the write-up, the demo, and the X
tiebreak. Every change re-clears the hard-requirement table before anything is called done.

Track ceiling-potential items continuously and never drop one. If something is hard-blocked by the
user, log it, state the gate ONCE, and move to the next item. Do not stop and do not spin holding
turns.

Attend to every character of the listing, not the gist. Each requirement, technique, trap and hint
is either delivered or logged as a deliberate non-goal with a reason. A dropped hint is a lost axis.

Never switch to an unrelated project to find work. If this session's work is genuinely exhausted or
entirely user-gated, say so plainly and stop.

## Done means
A showcase post linking the public repo, a demo at or under 3 minutes, a reproducible write-up, and
the injection transcript — backed by work a single-track competitor cannot approach. Winning is the
floor of the ambition, not the ceiling.
